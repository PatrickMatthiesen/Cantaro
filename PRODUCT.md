# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cantaro is for people who keep music and media libraries across multiple services and want one reliable place to manage, sync, and review them. Users are usually in focused library-management flows: finding items, checking progress, resolving observations, and keeping providers aligned.

## Product Purpose

Cantaro unifies music playlists, media watch progress, provider connections, and browser-extension observations into a personal archive. Success means users can trust Cantaro as the source of truth while still using their preferred external platforms.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, personal. The product should feel like a living archive of collected music and watched stories, with enough warmth to invite browsing and enough precision to make sync and provider state feel trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Cantaro should not look like a clone of Spotify, YouTube, AniList, Trakt, or a generic enterprise dashboard. Avoid cold gray admin UI, generic SaaS tropes, decorative glass effects, and provider branding that overwhelms Cantaro's own product shell.

## Design Principles

- Make provider complexity understandable instead of hiding it behind vague polish.
- Let artwork create richness while keeping page chrome calm and provider-independent.
- Treat violet as a state signal for current location, selection, focus, and key actions.
- Keep dense workflows scannable and predictable across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Preserve trust with clear empty, loading, error, sync, and review states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interactive controls should retain visible focus states, keyboard access, and reduced-motion alternatives. Color-coded sync, provider, and progress states must also be communicated with text or icons.
